This CIS Controls Implementation Plan outlines practical cybersecurity measures for a sole entrepreneur who creates and sells digital products through Gumroad while using social media platforms, primarily TikTok, to build an audience and generate sales. As the business relies heavily on online platforms, cloud services, and digital assets, it faces risks such as account compromise, phishing attacks, malware infections, data loss, vendor dependency, and business disruption.
The purpose of this document is to identify the most relevant CIS Controls for this business model and provide implementation recommendations to reduce cybersecurity risks, protect business assets, and support business continuity.
